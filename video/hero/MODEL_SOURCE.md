# 3D vehicle source

The active car model in `public/models/car-concept.glb` is [Khronos Car Concept](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/blob/main/Models/CarConcept/README.md), credited to Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH and distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is recolored to graphite for the video. `plate.svg` adds a fictional sample plate. `sports-car.glb` remains as an unused CC0 comparison asset from [3DAssets.dev](https://3dassets.dev/assets/car-park-and-road-vehicle-fleet-sports-car-900de487).

The earlier still-image composition uses AI-generated artwork. `FBSHero3D` instead renders the car model from a continuously moving virtual camera. The vehicle is a different design from the still-image concept.

Studio reflection lighting uses [Poly Haven Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09) by Sergej Majboroda under CC0 1.0.
